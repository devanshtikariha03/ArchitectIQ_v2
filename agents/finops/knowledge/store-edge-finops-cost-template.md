# Store Edge FinOps Cost Template

Version: 2026.07

Effective-Date: 2026-07-17

Tags: store-edge, pos, offline, hardware, rollout

## Controls
- Model store-edge hardware, POS lanes, HA pairs, local storage, device management, patching, monitoring, UPS/network resilience, field replacement, training, support desk load, and rollout waves.
- Include offline mode, reconciliation, conflict handling, local backups, WAN outage assumptions, and store operations support.
- Separate pilot, rollout, steady-state, replacement, support, and decommission cost.

## Risks
- Store rollout cost can be dominated by field operations, support, device lifecycle, training, and replacement logistics.
- Underfunded offline mode can increase transaction loss, reconciliation work, and support incidents.

## Validation Needed
- Confirm store count, POS lanes, offline mode, HA design, device lifecycle, field support rates, training effort, rollout wave plan, support desk assumptions, and replacement policy.

## Citations
- ArchitectIQ store-edge FinOps treats field operations and device lifecycle as mandatory cost drivers.
