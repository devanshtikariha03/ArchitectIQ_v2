# Cloud Support Licensing Partner Contingency Checklist
ID: cloud-support-licensing-partner-contingency-checklist
Version: 2026.07
Owner: ArchitectIQ FinOps Baseline
Effective-Date: 2026-07-17
Review-By: 2027-07-17
Tags: support, licensing, partner, non-prod, contingency

## Controls
- Cost enterprise support, SaaS seats, managed service support, marketplace subscriptions, partner implementation, rollout support, training, DR, non-prod parity, test environments, and contingency.
- Include observability, incident response, on-call, runbooks, game days, support access review, and operational readiness as budget lines.
- Treat support/licensing/partner costs as mandatory estimate components until an owner explicitly marks them out of scope.
- Apply contingency to assumption-heavy estimates and reduce it only when evidence matures.

## Risks
- Budget can appear feasible if support plans, partner effort, non-prod environments, licensing, and contingency are excluded.
- Enterprise customers often reject estimates that cover infrastructure but omit implementation and run costs.

## Validation Needed
- Confirm support tier, SaaS seats, partner rates, non-prod environments, DR parity, rollout support, training, managed service contracts, incident response needs, and contingency percentage.

## Citations
- ArchitectIQ enterprise FinOps estimates must include support, licensing, partner, non-prod, DR, and contingency cost lines.
