# Customer FinOps Policy Packs

Place customer-approved FinOps, procurement, cloud commercial, support, licensing, and budget-governance policies in this folder.

Each policy should use this structure:

```md
# Policy Title
ID: customer-finops-policy-id
Version: 2026.07
Owner: Customer FinOps Owner
Effective-Date: 2026-07-17
Review-By: 2027-07-17
Tags: budget, pricing, support, licensing

## Controls
- Customer-approved control.

## Risks
- Customer-specific budget or commercial risk.

## Validation Needed
- Evidence required before the agent can mark this policy verified.

## Citations
- Short customer-approved citation text safe to show in output.
```

Do not put secrets, contract numbers, confidential discounts, or account credentials in this folder. Use redacted policy excerpts and mark sensitive commercial terms as customer evidence required.
