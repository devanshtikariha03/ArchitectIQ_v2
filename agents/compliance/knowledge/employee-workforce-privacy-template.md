## Controls
- Identify employee, associate, badge, CCTV, productivity, device, and biometric data separately from customer data.
- Validate workforce privacy notice, access limits, retention, monitoring proportionality, region, and employee-rights workflows.

## Risks
- Workforce data can be incorrectly treated as operational telemetry and miss privacy, labour, and retention obligations.

## Validation Needed
- Confirm employee countries, workforce privacy notices, monitoring basis, retention, access reviews, and legal/HR owner approval.

## Citations
- ArchitectIQ workforce privacy baseline: staff monitoring and badge/CCTV data require separate owner validation.
