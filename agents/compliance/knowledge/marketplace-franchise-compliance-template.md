Tags: marketplace, franchise, seller, brand, partner, processor, data-sharing

## Controls
- Define controller/processor responsibility, data-sharing terms, brand/franchise boundary, seller/partner support access, export approvals, and incident obligations.
- Treat seller, franchise, logistics, and marketplace support access as processor or joint-processing paths until legal validates the role.

## Risks
- Marketplace or franchise data sharing can blur controller/processor responsibility and create unsupported cross-border exports.

## Validation Needed
- Confirm seller/franchise contracts, data-sharing agreements, processor role, support-access locations, export approvals, and breach responsibilities.

## Citations
- ArchitectIQ marketplace compliance baseline: partner and franchise data boundaries require explicit legal/privacy validation.
