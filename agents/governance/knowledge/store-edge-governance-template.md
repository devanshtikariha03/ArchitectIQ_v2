## Controls
- Store-edge rollout needs offline trading tests, queue replay evidence, device replacement runbooks, field support, store training, and wave stop/go criteria.
- Store operations must approve degraded-mode limits, local support process, and reconnect/reconciliation acceptance tests.

## Risks
- Store-edge changes can fail operationally even when central cloud services are healthy.

## Validation Needed
- Confirm pilot stores, field support owners, offline test results, queue replay evidence, and rollback criteria.
