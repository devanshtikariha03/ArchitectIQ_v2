# FinOps To Governance Handoff Template
ID: finops-to-governance-handoff-template
Version: 2026.07
Effective-Date: 2026-07-16
Review-By: 2027-07-16
Owner: FinOps Governance
Tags: finops, budget, unit-driver, pricing, cost-gate

## Controls
- Governance must keep budget feasibility, unit drivers, pricing evidence, support/licensing, non-prod parity, and contingency visible as approval gates.
- Any cost reduction that weakens security, compliance, resilience, or operations requires named risk acceptance.

## Risks
- Architecture can appear approved while cost assumptions remain unverified or tradeoffs weaken mandatory controls.

## Validation Needed
- Confirm budget owner, hard budget, unit drivers, pricing evidence status, and accepted cost/security/compliance tradeoffs.

## Citations
- ArchitectIQ FinOps governance baseline: budget feasibility is a governance gate, not only a FinOps estimate.
